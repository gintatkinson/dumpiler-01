## 1. Normative Statement

The projection engine of Subsystem 9 SHALL synthesize Epic specifications by partitioning models along top-level subsystem boundaries and projecting capability allocations.

---

## 2. Formal Invariant

Let $``\mathcal{S} = \{S_1, \dots, S_m\}``$ and $``\mathcal{M}_{\text{capabilities}} = \{c_1, \dots, c_n\}``$. Decomposition and allocation invariants satisfy:

```math
\begin{aligned}
E_k&=\{c\in\mathcal{M}_{\text{capabilities}}\mid\text{Alloc}(c)=S_k\},\quad k\in\{1,\dots,m\} \\
\bigcup_{k=1}^m E_k&=\mathcal{M}_{\text{capabilities}}\land(\forall i\ne j,\,E_i\cap E_j=\emptyset) \\
\text{Allocation}(c)&=\langle\text{name}(c),\text{FQN}(\text{Alloc}(c)),\text{doc}(c)\rangle \\
\text{Hierarchy}(S_k)&=\text{TreeDecomp}(S_k,D)=\langle V_k,E_k,\text{depth}\le D\rangle \\
(\exists c:\text{Alloc}(c)=\bot\lor\text{Alloc}(c)\notin\mathcal{S}&\lor\exists i\ne j:c\in E_i\cap E_j)\implies\text{Diagnostic}(c,\text{E0303})\in\mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}}\land c\gets\bot_{\text{epic}} \\
(\text{HasCycle}(\text{Hierarchy}(S_k))&\lor\text{depth}(S_k)\gt D)\implies\text{Diagnostic}(S_k,\text{E0300})\in\mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}}\land S_k\gets\bot_{\text{epic}} \\
|\mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}}|\ge C_{\text{max\_errors}}&\implies\text{Diagnostic}(\text{E0199})\in\mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}},\quad\Delta_x=\text{UUIDv5}(\text{OID}_{\text{DEAP}},\text{FQN}(x))
\end{aligned}
```

| Symbol / Term | Domain / Formal Definition |
| :--- | :--- |
| $``\mathcal{S}, S_k``$ | Architectural subsystems and subsystem node $k$ |
| $``\mathcal{M}_{\text{capabilities}}, c``$ | SysML v2 capability universe and capability element |
| $``E_k, \text{Alloc}(c)``$ | Epic partition $k$ and capability allocation mapping |
| $``\text{Hierarchy}(S_k), D``$ | Structural decomposition tree and depth limit $D$ |
| $``\bot_{\text{epic}}, \mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}}, C_{\text{max\_errors}}``$ | Sentinel, diagnostic sink, and error limit |
| $``\Delta_x, \text{OID}_{\text{DEAP}}``$ | UUIDv5 anchor and canonical namespace OID |

## 3. Computational Complexity & Algorithmic Bounds

- **Complexity Class:** Class $\mathbf{P}$ (Deterministic Polynomial Time).
- **Asymptotic Bound:** $O(|S| \cdot D)$ tree decomposition traversal and $O(|C| + |S|)$ capability synthesis ($|S| = |\mathcal{S}|$, depth $D$, $``|C| = |\mathcal{M}_{\text{capabilities}}|``$). Work-stealing pool scales as $``O((|S| \cdot D + |C|)/N_{\text{max\_workers}} + \log N_{\text{max\_workers}})``$. Space: $O(|S| \cdot D + |C|)$; buffer: $O(1)$.
- **Tractable Subclass and Frontier:** N/A (Deterministic P). Tree traversal avoids NP-2 packing (REQ-0147) and NP-8 solving (REQ-0197).

## 4. Verification & Conformance Criteria (IEEE 29148 Acceptance Criteria)

### AC-01: Structural Decomposition and Capability Projection
- **Given:** Subsystem `Subsystem_0` with `Part_0`, `Port_0` ($D = 3$), and `Capability_0`.
- **When:** Lowering Epic `Epic_Alpha`.
- **Then:** Emits `Epic_Alpha` with structural tree, capability table, and UUIDv5 anchors.
- **Diagnostic:** Diagnostic sink remains empty.

### AC-02: Partition Completeness and Disjointness
- **Given:** Model with 5 subsystems and 50 capabilities (10 per subsystem).
- **When:** Partitioning capabilities across Epics.
- **Then:** Confirms $``\bigcup_{k=0}^4 E_k = \mathcal{M}_{\text{capabilities}}``$ and $``\forall i \ne j, E_i \cap E_j = \emptyset``$.
- **Diagnostic:** Diagnostic sink remains empty.

### AC-03: Allocation Violation Rejection
- **Given:** Capability `Capability_Alpha` unallocated or mapped to `Subsystem_0` and `Subsystem_1`.
- **When:** Validating allocation partitions.
- **Then:** Rejects model, emits `E0303`, and binds sentinel $``\bot_{\text{epic}}``$.
- **Diagnostic:** `E0303` (CapabilityAllocationViolation).

### AC-04: Decomposition Cycle and Delimiter Recovery
- **Given:** Subsystem `Subsystem_0` with cyclic nesting ($> D$) and undeclared targets, separated by `;`.
- **When:** Delimiter recovery processes faults.
- **Then:** Emits `E0300` and `E0303`, recovers on `;`, and halts at $``C_{\text{max\_errors}}``$ with `E0199`.
- **Diagnostic:** `E0300`, `E0303`, `E0199`.
